Real Free Soccer Online and Mobile Privacy Policy

I. Introduction

Real Free Soccer knows that you care how information about you is used and shared, and we appreciate your trust that we will do so carefully and sensibly. We respect the privacy rights of consumers and recognize the importance of protecting the information collected about you.

We have adopted this global online Privacy Policy to explain how we store and use personal and non-personal information we collect online on our websites, during your use of our online products and services, including online gameplay, and on mobile platforms. This policy does not cover information provided online in response to job postings.

II. What Is Personal Information and When Does Real Free Soccer Collect It?

Real Free Soccer collects both personal and non-personal consumer information. Personal information is information that identifies you and that can be used alone to contact you online or offline.

Real Free Soccer may collect personal information from online visitors during contest registration and prize acceptance, warranty registration and requests, customer support and technical service requests, player matchup and other online competitions, registration for games or special events, newsletter subscriptions, referral services, marketing surveys, email campaigns, creation of a personal profile, product or subscription orders, third party service requests, access to our products or services on social networks or other third party services, or otherwise through use of our software, mobile, or online services where personal information is required.

Information collected will vary depending on the activity and may include your name, email address, phone number, home address, birth date, mobile phone number, and credit card information. Prize winners may be required to provide identification information for tax purposes, and that information will be used only for prize fulfillment.

III. What Happens to the Information Real Free Soccer Collects?

A. How Real Free Soccer Uses Your Information

Real Free Soccer uses your information to fulfill your specific requests and purchase orders, send purchase confirmation and other account-related information, improve our products and services, understand user behavior and preferences, troubleshoot technical problems, serve advertising, authenticate users, enforce our Terms of Service, and ensure proper functioning of our products and services.

B. Will Real Free Soccer Share My Information With Third Parties?

Real Free Soccer will never share your personally identifiable information with third parties without your consent. We may share non-personally identifiable, aggregated, or public information with third parties. We may also use third party contractors to provide services such as email delivery, prize fulfillment, contest administration, credit card processing, shipping, analytics, or market research. These third parties are required to use data consistently with this policy.

IV. How Does Real Free Soccer Protect Your Personal Information?

Real Free Soccer understands the importance of keeping your information safe and secure. We make commercially reasonable efforts to protect your personal information and ensure the security of our systems. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100 percent secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

V. Third Party Sites

Our website may contain advertising or services that link to other websites such as Twitter, Facebook, and YouTube. Because we cannot control the activities of third parties, we cannot accept responsibility for any use of your personal information by such third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third party website before providing personal information.

VI. Data Deletion

To delete your account or data in Soccer Superstar, you can take either one of the following actions:

  • Sign in to your Facebook account in the game, then go to Settings, Data Management, Data Deletion.
  • Go to https://sss.simface.com/ and follow the instructions.
