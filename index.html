FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Is Soccer Superstar free to play?

Yes. It's free to download and play on iOS and Android. The game has optional in-app purchases and ads.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Leagues, tournaments and training all work offline, so once the game is installed you can play anywhere.

What devices is it available on?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play, iPhone and iPad through the App Store, and Amazon Fire tablets through the Amazon Appstore. It's rated Everyone.

How do the controls work?

Swipe across the ball to shoot. The direction, speed and curve of your swipe decide where the ball goes. There are no buttons or joysticks.

How do I unlock new players?

Playing the main levels earns you reward packs and player shards. Once you have enough shards for a player, they join your squad.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Despite the name, it's a limited-time event that comes around about once a month. Each one has its own themed stadium and exclusive gear. Finish it before the timer runs out to get the rewards.
