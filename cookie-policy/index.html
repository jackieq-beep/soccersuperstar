Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Soccer Superstar website at www.simface.com (the "Website"), operated by Real Freestyle Soccer ("we", "us" or "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies. It applies to the Website only. For the Soccer Superstar game, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, let a website remember information on your device in the same way. In this policy, "cookies" covers both.

2. Cookies we use

The Website does not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking tools, social media pixels, or any other third-party cookies. The fonts and images on the Website are served from our own domain, so loading a page does not send your information to font or image providers.

The Website saves only two small items on your device:

NameTypePurposeHow long it is kept
ss-lang Local storage (first party) Remembers the language you picked in the language menu, so the Website opens in that language next time. It stores only a language code such as "es" or "ja". Until you clear your browser data
ss-notice Local storage (first party) Remembers that you closed the cookie notice, so it is not shown again. It stores only the value "1". Until you clear your browser data

These items are set only when you choose a language or close the notice. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are not used to identify or track you. Because they are strictly necessary to provide features you asked for, we do not ask for consent before storing them.

3. Website hosting

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. We do not receive these logs and do not use them. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

4. Links to other websites

The Website links to Google Play, the App Store, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. These sites set their own cookies when you visit them, under their own cookie and privacy policies. Nothing from these sites is loaded on our pages until you click a link.

5. How to manage or delete cookies

You can remove these items at any time by clearing your browser's site data for www.simface.com, or by clearing your browsing data. Most browsers also let you block websites from saving data. The Website still works if you do this. You will just need to pick your language again, and the cookie notice will show again.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add new cookies, for example to measure how many people visit the Website, we will update this policy first, and we will ask for your consent before setting any cookie that is not strictly necessary. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, email us at soccersuperstarteam@gmail.com.
